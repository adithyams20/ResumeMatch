Senior Machine Learning Engineer — FinTech Platform

Company: PayStream Technologies
Location: Remote (US/EU)
Type: Full-Time

ABOUT THE ROLE
PayStream is building the next generation of real-time fraud detection and
credit-risk scoring systems. We are looking for a Senior Machine Learning
Engineer with at least 5 years of experience to design, train, and operate
ML models at scale in a regulated financial environment.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design and implement production-grade ML pipelines for fraud detection and
  credit scoring, processing millions of transactions per day.
- Collaborate with data engineers to ensure high-quality feature engineering
  and data pipelines built on Apache Spark and Kafka.
- Deploy and monitor models using MLflow, BentoML, or similar MLOps tooling.
- Champion model explainability (SHAP, LIME) to meet regulatory requirements
  (SOC 2, GDPR).
- Mentor junior engineers and conduct technical design reviews.
- Participate in on-call rotation for production ML systems.

REQUIREMENTS
- 5+ years of professional experience in software engineering or machine learning.
- Strong proficiency in Python and hands-on experience with scikit-learn,
  XGBoost, LightGBM, or PyTorch.
- Experience building and maintaining real-time data pipelines with Kafka or
  Spark Streaming.
- Solid knowledge of MLOps practices: experiment tracking (MLflow), model
  serving, A/B testing, and monitoring for data drift.
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS or GCP) and container orchestration
  (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Familiarity with SQL and columnar data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift).
- Strong understanding of software engineering best practices: testing (pytest),
  CI/CD, and code review.

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience with LLMs, RAG architectures, or LangChain for AI-powered features.
- Knowledge of streaming databases or time-series stores (InfluxDB, TimescaleDB).
- Familiarity with graph databases (Neo4j) for fraud network analysis.
- Contributions to open-source ML projects.

COMPENSATION
- \$160,000 – \$200,000 base salary depending on experience.
- Equity, health benefits, and a generous learning budget.
